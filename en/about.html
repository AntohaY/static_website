About Us

Luc Electro Pro is built around quality electrical work and thoughtful Loxone integration.

We focus on projects where dependable electrical installations and smart home functionality need to be treated as one connected solution, not two separate conversations.

Main Focus

Electrical installations and Loxone smart home integration for modern residential and commercial spaces.

Service Area

Cluj-Napoca, surrounding areas, and selected projects nationwide across Romania.

Company

Luc Electro Pro SRL, based in Floresti, Cluj County.

Who We Are

A company shaped by practical installations and modern smart living needs.

Our work starts with the essentials: correct electrical planning, reliable circuits, clear execution, and a finished installation that performs well over time.

From there, we help clients extend that foundation into Loxone smart home integration, bringing lighting, climate, shading, and comfort logic together in a way that feels natural to use.
